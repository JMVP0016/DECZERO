# HAWAPACHA: privacy

HAWAPACHA is a student project by team DecZero for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.
It is not a NASA product.

## What happens when you sign in

Signing in with a Google account is optional. It is only needed to publish a report
and to follow your own reports.

- Google tells the app your e-mail address so that the app can recognise your account.
- The app does **not** store or publish your e-mail address, your name or your picture.
- The app keeps an anonymous identifier of your account (a salted hash of the e-mail
  address, from which the address cannot be read) and the nickname you choose.
- A cookie in your browser keeps you signed in. Signing out removes it.

## What is public

- The nickname you choose.
- Every report you publish: its text, the sky position, the date and time, and the
  review steps. Reports are stored as public issues of this repository.

Do not write personal data in a nickname or in a report.

## Reviewers

Reviewers are chosen by the team. Their name and role, as agreed with them, are
published with every step they sign.

## Removing your data

Write to the team through the issues of this repository and ask for your reports or
your nickname to be removed.

## Other services

The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud and stores reports with GitHub. Sign-in is
provided by Google. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.
